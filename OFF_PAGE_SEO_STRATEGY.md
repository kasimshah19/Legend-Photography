# Off-Page SEO & Authority Strategy

This document outlines legitimate, white-hat methods for building external authority for Legend Photography. **Never purchase backlinks or participate in link schemes.**

## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP)
*   Ensure NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency between the website (`siteConfig.ts`) and GBP.
*   Regularly upload high-quality portfolio images to the GBP listing.
*   Encourage legitimate client reviews directly on the Google listing.

## 2. Vendor Collaborations
*   When publishing a real wedding story, credit and link to the venue, makeup artist, florist, and event planner.
*   Share the gallery with these vendors and request that they link back to your website if they feature the photos on their own blogs or portfolios.

## 3. Venue Directories & Features
*   Identify preferred vendor lists for popular local wedding venues.
*   Submit real weddings to local or national wedding publications (e.g., WedMeGood, ShaadiSaga, or regional equivalents) for editorial features.

## 4. Social Media Synergy
*   Ensure Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube profiles contain the exact production URL.
*   Use YouTube descriptions (if publishing wedding films) to link directly to the corresponding `/portfolio/[slug]` story on the website.

## 5. Professional Memberships
*   Join legitimate photography associations or local chambers of commerce. Ensure directory profiles point to the official website domain.
