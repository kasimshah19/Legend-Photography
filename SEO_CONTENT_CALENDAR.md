# SEO Content Calendar & Publishing Cadence

This calendar provides a realistic schedule for publishing high-quality, real-world content to build topical authority over time. Quality must always take precedence over quantity.

## Month 1: Foundation & Authority
*   **Week 2**: Publish 1 Real Wedding Story to `/portfolio/[slug]`. Ensure rich description, location context, and robust image alt tags.
*   **Week 4**: Publish Informational Guide: *"How to Choose a Wedding Photographer"*. Link to portfolio and contact pages.

## Month 2: Pre-Wedding & Venue Focus
*   **Week 2**: Publish 1 Real Pre-Wedding Story to `/portfolio/[slug]`. Highlight location and styling.
*   **Week 4**: Publish Venue Spotlight (if applicable): A real wedding shot at a prominent local venue, highlighting the studio's familiarity with the location.

## Month 3: Expanding Services
*   **Week 2**: Publish 1 Maternity Story to `/portfolio/[slug]`.
*   **Week 4**: Publish Informational Guide: *"Pre-Wedding Shoot Ideas and Preparation"*.

## Rules for Publishing
1.  **Never Use AI Filler**: Content must reflect actual business experience and philosophy.
2.  **Visual First**: Every post must feature premium, optimized photography.
3.  **Internal Linking**: Always link new content to the core `/services` and `/contact` pages.
